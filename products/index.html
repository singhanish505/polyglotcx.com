Products

One platform. A product line for the whole contact center.

PolyglotCX is a provider-agnostic contact center & customer experience platform. It started with automated quality management — still the flagship — and the same backend now carries a growing set of products for agents, supervisors and the QA desk. Each is labelled by stage so you always know what is available today.

AI Quality Management

Automated QA for 100% of calls, in any language. An eight-stage pipeline turns every call into a transcript, a weighted QA score with a transcript quote behind every criterion, four role-specific summaries, a CRM ticket and a coaching plan — plus vision-based screen-behaviour scoring and a rule engine that auto-approves, flags or escalates.

100% coverage vs the 1–3% manual norm — ~2 minutes to review, not 20
40+ languages auto-detected, code-switching included
Screen Behaviour QA, Coaching Hub and a multi-tenant console

One platform underneath

Every product, the same backbone

Provider-agnostic
A Contact Center Abstraction Layer over built-in SIP, Amazon Connect, Genesys, NICE CXone, Talkdesk, Five9 and Dynamics 365 — products never branch on the vendor.
Multi-tenant core
Strict per-organisation isolation, token quotas and billing, BYOK keys encrypted at rest, per-tenant model configuration.
One security model
MFA, per-tenant OIDC SSO, RBAC, tamper-evident audit, data-residency routing and pre-LLM PII redaction — shared by every product.
Shared AI services
The same grounding, copilot and retrieval services feed QA scoring, the in-call copilot and agent-desktop wrap-up.
