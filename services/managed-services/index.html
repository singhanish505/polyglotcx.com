Managed Services

Your quality function, run for you

Don’t have a QA team — or want yours focused on coaching, not scoring? Our specialists operate PolyglotCX on your behalf: calibration, scorecard tuning, weekly insight reports and compliance escalation.

What we operate

QA coverage without the headcount

Platform operations
We run ingestion, monitor pipeline health, manage custom vocabulary and keep your scorecards aligned to evolving business rules.
Human calibration
Our QA specialists review AI evaluations weekly, calibrate scores, and tune rule-engine thresholds so auto-approval stays trustworthy.
Insight reporting
Weekly executive summaries: agent leaderboards, compliance trends, coaching priorities and root-cause themes across your queues.
Compliance watch
High-severity risks are triaged and escalated to your named contacts within agreed SLAs — with the evidence quote attached.
Coaching program design
We design and run coaching programs in the Coaching Hub — enrollment, session cadence and action-item follow-through.
Onboarding & migration
From telephony connection to first scored call in days. We handle source integration, scorecard build and team training.
